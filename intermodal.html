Why Intermodal

The Right Move for Your Domestic Cargo

Intermodal shipping combines the flexibility of trucking with the cost advantages of rail, delivering significant savings for longer domestic lanes while maintaining schedule reliability.

  • Cost Savings: Rail portion reduces fuel costs and driver hours on long-haul lanes
  • Capacity: Access to rail capacity relieves truck driver shortage pressure
  • Reliability: Scheduled rail departures provide consistent transit times
  • Visibility: Full tracking from pickup through final delivery at every handoff
  • Sustainability: Rail moves 4x more cargo per gallon than highway trucking
