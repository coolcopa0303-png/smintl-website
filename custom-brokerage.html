Custom Brokerage

Expert US customs clearance with full regulatory compliance — from electronic entry filing to FDA, DOT, EPA, and USDA declarations.

Expert Clearance

Navigate US Customs with Confidence

Our licensed customs brokers help you choose optimal modes of transportation and appropriate carriers. They provide full assistance to importers in assigning shipments the best routes, cost estimates, and ensuring full regulatory compliance.

We handle electronic entry filing through ABI (Automated Brokerage Interface), ensuring fast and accurate clearance for all your imports and exports across the United States. Our team stays current with all regulatory changes so you don't have to.

From straightforward commercial imports to complex multi-agency declarations, we manage the paperwork so you can focus on your business.

Full Service Offering

What We Handle

Comprehensive customs brokerage services covering every aspect of import and export compliance.

📋
Electronic Entry Filing
Automated Brokerage Interface (ABI) for fast, accurate electronic entry filing. Remote location filing for maximum flexibility.
🔍
Classification & Compliance
HTS tariff classification, binding ruling requests, and full regulatory compliance review to avoid fines and delays.
📦
ISF Processing
Importer Security Filing (10+2) submitted accurately and on time. Quota pre-processing and monitoring services included.
🛡️
Customs Bonds & Insurance
Single-entry and continuous customs bonds. Comprehensive cargo insurance with post-entry amendments and reconciliation.
📝
Multi-Agency Declarations
FDA, DOT, EPA, USDA, and CPSC declarations handled expertly. We manage prior notices, holds, and agency examinations.
🌐
Temporary Import Bonds
TIBs for trade shows, exhibitions, and manufacturing. Carnet processing and in-bond movement management.
📊
Duty Optimization
FTA analysis, first-sale valuation, and duty drawback programs to legally minimize your import duty obligations.
⚡
Expedited Releases
CTPAT-facilitated clearance, PGA accelerated review, and direct delivery coordination for time-sensitive shipments.
Our Process

How We Clear Your Cargo

A streamlined four-step process that gets your goods moving quickly and compliantly.

1
Document Review

We review your commercial invoice, packing list, B/L, and any special permits or certificates required for your cargo.

2
Entry Preparation

Our brokers classify your goods under the correct HTS code and prepare all CBP entry documents via ABI electronic filing.

3
Agency Clearance

We file all required multi-agency declarations (FDA, USDA, EPA, etc.) and manage any examination or hold requests.

4
Release & Delivery

Once released, we coordinate with your carrier or trucker for immediate pickup and deliver full documentation to your team.
