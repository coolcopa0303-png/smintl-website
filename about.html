About Us

Over a decade of building trusted logistics partnerships across the United States and beyond.

Our Story

Built on Relationships,
Driven by Precision

Sen Mart International was founded in 2013 with a clear mission: to provide the best and most personalized service to every client in the trade community. While the company started lean, innovative operational techniques enabled almost immediate success and steady, sustained growth across the US market.

From day one, our philosophy was different from large commodity freight brokers. We take time to learn each client's specific business, supply chain structure, peak seasons, and compliance requirements — then build a service program tailored to their exact needs.

That commitment has paid off. We have established a record of building long-term and mutually beneficial relationships with our clients. Many have been with us since our founding, and many more were referred by those who already trust us with their cargo.

What Drives Us

Four principles that guide every decision and every shipment at Sen Mart International.

Personalized Service

Every client is different. We build relationships, not just transactions — understanding your business to provide service that actually fits your needs.

Compliance & Accuracy

Precision in documentation prevents costly delays. Our brokers are meticulous — every entry, every declaration, every deadline met without exception.

24/7 Availability
