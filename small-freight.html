Small Freight

Flexible LTL and parcel solutions for smaller shipments — cost-effective consolidation without sacrificing speed or reliability.

Small Parcel & LTL

Every Shipment Deserves the Same Attention

Not every shipment fills a full container or truck. Sen Mart International offers flexible small freight and Less-than-Truckload (LTL) solutions designed to move smaller consignments cost-effectively without sacrificing speed or reliability.

Whether you need a single pallet, a few boxes, or a small courier delivery, our network of carriers and consolidation services ensure your small shipment receives the same attention and care as a full container load.

Our three US warehouse locations (LAX, JFK, DFW) serve as consolidation hubs — we combine your smaller loads with others moving in the same direction, dramatically reducing your per-unit shipping cost.

  • Less-than-Truckload (LTL) shipping — domestic nationwide
  • Small parcel & courier delivery for individual boxes and packages
  • Consolidation service — merge loads for significant cost savings
  • UPS Ground and FedEx Freight nationwide distribution
  • Local pickup & last-mile delivery from our warehouse network
  • Competitive pricing optimized for smaller load volumes

When to Use Small Freight

The Right Solution for Smaller Loads

Small freight services fill the gap between courier packages and full truckloads — ideal for many common shipping scenarios.

Import Consolidation

Ocean or air imports that are too small for a full container? We deconsolidate at our warehouse and distribute directly to your recipients or facilities.

Domestic Replenishment

Regular smaller shipments between your facilities, stores, or to Amazon FBA warehouses. Scheduled pickups keep your inventory flowing.

Urgent Sample Shipments

Product samples, trade show materials, and time-sensitive small lots that need guaranteed delivery without paying full truck rates.
