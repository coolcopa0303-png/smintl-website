Freight Forwarding

Specialized air and ocean transportation forwarding your shipments to and from all destinations worldwide — with competitive pricing and personalized service.

Air Freight

Fast, Reliable Air Cargo Solutions

Our well-trained and experienced team strives to find the most cost-efficient solutions to forward your freight by air. Sen Mart acts as Sales and House Agent for many airlines, ensuring the best forwarding solutions for your cargo from origin to destination.

Whether you need regular scheduled service or premium expedited handling for urgent shipments, our air freight team delivers with precision and speed across all major global routes.

  • Competitive transportation pricing through airline partnerships
  • Premium expedited service for urgent and time-sensitive shipments
  • Custom clearance & documentation handling
  • Special courier service for high-value or sensitive cargo
  • Consolidation service (LCL equivalent for air)
  • Door-to-door delivery worldwide
  • Real-time cargo tracking & trace
Ocean Freight

Full-Scope Ocean Freight Services

From consolidated shipments to Full Container Loads, from conventional cargo to breakbulk, from oversized project cargo to temperature-controlled reefers — Sen Mart handles all shipments with high-frequency sailing schedules and a global agency network.

Our ocean freight team manages every detail from booking to documentation, ensuring smooth transit across all major shipping lanes worldwide.

  • Competitive ocean freight pricing — FCL & LCL
  • Import & Export — both directions, all origins and destinations
  • Customs clearance & complete documentation support
  • Project cargo — oversized, heavy lift, breakbulk
  • Refrigerated (reefer) & dry van container options
  • High-frequency sailing schedule across all major trade lanes
